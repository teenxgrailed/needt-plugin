# Resale and inventory economics

Use for buying goods to resell. No fixed rates live here — verify current fees and taxes.
Calculate immediately on labeled assumptions; do not hold the verdict hostage to missing
inputs. Ask only for the input that would flip the verdict.

## Before the numbers: are the goods real?

For branded, hyped or luxury goods, authenticity decides more than margin. Red flags: a bulk
lot of one model from one seller, price far under completed sales, stock photos, no tags or
receipts. Request per-item photos of labels, tags, hardware, serials/QR or certificates, and
check against known authentication points or a paid authentication service. Unverified
authenticity → the verdict is conditional on it, stated first.

## Real cost

Purchase, buyer-protection or platform buy fees, inbound freight, FX spread, import taxes and
handling where applicable, inspection or repair, packaging, listing costs.

Cross-border check: when the buy region, the seller's location and the main buyer region
differ (e.g. EU marketplace → non-EU seller → US buyers), add import VAT on arrival, export
shipping per item, and the buyer's import duties, which push achievable prices down. These
often move the verdict more than platform fees. Separate cash outlay
from anything recoverable later.

## Real proceeds per item

Realistic selling price from **completed** comparable sales (not asking prices), minus platform
fee, payment processing, seller-paid outbound shipping, expected return or defect cost.
If completed sales are behind a login or unavailable, use the best observable proxy, label it,
and widen the downside case.

## Recovery and downside

For similar items with positive net proceeds:

```
cash-recovery units = ceil(total cash outlay / net cash per sold unit)
```

This is recovery of cash, not profit. Zero or negative net → no recovery point. Recovery units
above lot size → the lot cannot recover its cash in that scenario; say so plainly.
Mixed lots: calculate per item and sale mix, not an imaginary average item.

Run planned, lower-price and lower-sell-through cases; label stress assumptions as scenarios,
not forecasts. Keep unsold stock separate from cash received; do not value it at full price.

## Capital and verdict

Weigh profit against time to recover cash, effort, available funds, and what the locked money
displaces. Do not assert an alternative buy is available without evidence.

Deliver: verdict (take / take if X / walk), the input that flips it, and the next concrete step
(e.g. the exact photo request to send the seller — drafted, not sent).
